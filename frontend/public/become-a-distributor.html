Who can apply

Distribution of pharmaceutical products in India is regulated. To partner as a Fair Ford distributor you should have:

A valid drug licence

A current wholesale drug licence to stock and distribute pharmaceutical products in your state.

GST registration

Active GST registration for compliant, invoiced B2B supply.

Territory presence

An established distribution or stockist operation serving retail chemists in a defined area.

Distributor FAQ

Who can become a Fair Ford distributor?

Licensed pharmaceutical distributors and stockists with a valid drug licence and GST registration who serve a defined territory in India can apply for a distributor partnership.

How do I apply to become a distributor?

Submit the distributor application form with your business and KYC details. Our team reviews and verifies the application, and on approval your account is activated with distributor pricing.

How are orders assigned to distributors?

Retailer orders are routed automatically to the nearest serviceable distributor based on the shop's pincode and the distributor's territory, and you are notified by email when an order lands in your area.

What products can I distribute?

The full Fair Ford catalogue of 165+ pharmaceutical and wellness formulations across tablets, capsules, syrups, injections, drops, ointments, sachets, vitamins and personal care, at distributor pricing that unlocks on approval.
