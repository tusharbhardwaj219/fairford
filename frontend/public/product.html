Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our procurement partners ask most. Anything else — our team replies within one business day.

Talk to our team
Who can order on this platform?

The marketplace is for pharmaceutical businesses — retailers, chemists, stockists, distributors, hospitals and institutional buyers. Anyone can browse the catalogue and see MRP; ordering requires a registered account with approved KYC.

Why can't I see trade prices?

Trade pricing is confidential and role-specific. Retailer and distributor rates are released by the server only after you sign in with an approved account — they are never present in the public page, so they cannot be read by inspecting it.

How do I register as a retailer?

Use the retailer registration form and upload your drug licence, GST certificate and supporting KYC documents. Once our team verifies them, your account is approved and trade pricing unlocks across the catalogue.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Any product carrying an MOQ shows it on the product card and detail page. Where no MOQ is shown, the product can be ordered from a single unit. For large or institutional volumes, request wholesale pricing and we will quote against your requirement.

How are orders delivered?

Each order is automatically routed to the nearest distributor or stockist that services your shop's pincode or city, who then fulfils and dispatches it. If your area is not yet covered, checkout tells you at once rather than accepting an order we cannot serve.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, or online payment by UPI, card, netbanking and wallets through our secure gateway. Every order is invoiced with GST applied per line item.
