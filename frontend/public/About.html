Who we are

A pharmaceutical company that both makes and moves medicine.

Fair Ford Pharmaceuticals is a manufacturing and distribution company based in Faridabad, Haryana. We produce pharmaceutical and wellness products to WHO-GMP standards and supply them across India through our own distribution network.

We serve the whole chain — distributors, stockists, chemists, hospitals, institutions and households — with one connected operation: manufacturing, quality control, warehousing, distribution and a digital ordering platform that routes every order to the nearest serviceable stockist.

Because we run manufacturing and distribution together, partners get a single, accountable source for genuine, quality-controlled products — with GST-compliant billing and flexible payment on every order.

Manufacturing & quality

Quality is a process, not a claim.

Our products are manufactured to WHO-GMP standards under the applicable Indian pharmaceutical regulations, with quality control at every stage — because the strip on the shelf is only as trustworthy as the process behind it.

Manufacturing and compliance documentation — including Certificates of Analysis — is shared with verified partners and institutional buyers on request.

Multiple dosage forms

Tablets, capsules, syrups, ointments, creams, drops, powders and nutraceuticals.

Multi-stage QC

Quality checks through production and before every dispatch.

Batch records & COA

Batch documentation kept; Certificate of Analysis available on request.

Regulatory compliance

Operated under applicable CDSCO / Schedule M pharmaceutical regulations.
